## 💡 Hypotheses & interpretations

- The 15.1% location rate (128,541 / 849,600) reflects the challenge of locating surface events with enveloc — many clusters likely don't pass SNR or multi-station thresholds required for a reliable location. This is expected behavior for the enveloc pipeline on diffuse surface events.
- The high SU confusion with PX (ambiguous_su_px: 254; confused_px: 138) and vice versa (ambiguous_px_su: 270; confused_su: 60) suggests the SU/PX boundary remains the hardest classification problem for QuakeXNet — the missed_events.csv (307 events) is the right place to look for systematic failure modes.
- The ceed dataset (4.14M traces, 100% P+S labeling) dwarfs all other SeisBench datasets and may dominate any benchmark if not carefully subsampled. The pnw dataset's 100% P+S completeness is a strength for local benchmarking.
- The growing station count (10 in 2010 → 41 in 2024) means raw per-year event counts are not directly comparable across the catalog — network sensitivity is a confound that needs to be accounted for in any temporal trend analysis.
- The infrasound FK location error of 8.34 km is moderate; this could reflect array aperture limitations or velocity model mismatch rather than a fundamental problem with the approach.
